gmail-mcp

A private tool that lets its owner work with their own Gmail account from Claude Code, an AI assistant, on their own computer.

What it is

gmail-mcp is a local Model Context Protocol (MCP) server. It runs only on the owner's computer and connects a single Gmail account, the owner's, to Claude Code. It is built on the open-source server-gmail-autoauth-mcp package.

Who can use it

Only the owner. gmail-mcp is not offered to the public: there is no sign-up, no hosted service, and no other people's accounts are connected.

What it does

  • Searches and reads messages when the owner asks.
  • Writes drafts, and sends email only on the owner's explicit request.
  • Applies labels and manages Gmail filters.
  • Saves an attachment to the owner's computer when asked to.

Google permissions it requests

ScopeWhy it is needed
gmail.modifyRead, search, draft, send and label messages.
gmail.settings.basicCreate and manage Gmail filters.

Your data

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. gmail-mcp has no server or database, and it does not sell Gmail data or share it for advertising. The Privacy Policy describes exactly what is accessed and where it goes.
